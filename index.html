You've thought about this

Will you ask about my immigration status?

Never. We don't ask, and it doesn't affect your case. Your employer broke the law. Not you. Filing a complaint does not affect visa or green card applications.

What if my boss finds out and retaliates?

Retaliation is illegal. If they fire you, cut your hours, or threaten you for speaking up, that's a separate violation. It makes your case stronger, not weaker.

How much does this cost?

Nothing upfront. We only get paid if we recover money for you. If we don't win, you owe nothing. The money we recover goes to your family, not to lawyers.

I'm paid in cash. No pay stubs. Can you still help?

Yes. Cash payments, no records, no formal schedule. None of that stops us. It actually shows your employer was already hiding what they were doing.

Is my case too small to matter?

If they owe you money, it matters. That's money for rent, for food, for your children, for the family back home. We've recovered thousands for workers who thought the same thing.

Do I have to go to court?

Most cases settle without trial. We handle the paperwork, the calls, the negotiations. You don't face your employer alone. We do that for you.
